Logs go to {{ .Values.loki.url }} with cluster="{{ .Values.clusterName }}".

The Alloy Operator turns the Alloy object into a DaemonSet within a minute or so:

  kubectl get alloys.collectors.grafana.com -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}
  kubectl get daemonset,pods -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}

Check a collector is tailing files and pushing successfully:

  kubectl port-forward -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} daemonset/{{ .Values.collector.name }} 12345:12345
  curl -s localhost:12345/metrics | grep -E 'loki_source_file_files_active_total|loki_write_request_duration_seconds_count'
